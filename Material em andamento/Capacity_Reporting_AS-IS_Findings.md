## Purpose of This Dimension

Capacity & Reporting is the **missing fifth dimension** in the baseline AS-IS matrix. Without it, the Value-Stream Map shows HOW work flows but NOT whether the organization has the resources to absorb it or the visibility to manage it. This dimension converts the flow view into a capacity-aware, data-aware view — prerequisite for TOM sizing and KPI design.

## Cross-Team Patterns (Capacity & Reporting Dimension)

### Pattern 1 — No Formal Capacity Planning
4 of 6 teams (GIS, Corporate, G&E, Kumar SAP) lack formal capacity planning. Only Global SAP has a clear quantitative view (5+4–6 FTE, turnover acknowledged). **Consequence**: no branch-level capacity reconciliation; trade-offs are invisible.

### Pattern 2 — Knowledge Concentration is Universal
Every team reports knowledge concentrated in individuals (Kumar SAP: team members · Corporate: Sarath · G&E: Dan · Global SAP: 20-yr tenure + Vanessa). **Consequence**: key-person risk is the single most repeated theme across the Capacity dimension.

### Pattern 3 — Reporting Is Tool-Fragmented and Person-Dependent
Every team uses ≥2 tools (IT Now + Jira + OneNote + spreadsheets + PPM Now). None has a single source of truth. Several admit "check with [Dan/Vanessa/Sarath]" — reporting is not self-service.

### Pattern 4 — Metrics Gap Is Admitted, Not Hidden
Two teams (GIS, Kumar SAP) explicitly state they have no flow/process KPIs. One team (G&E) states "we don't have visibility." Global SAP has 1 KPI (incident count). **Consequence**: the organization cannot measure delivery performance, cycle time, throughput, or WIP — preventing any data-driven prioritization.

### Pattern 5 — Accenture / Vendor Dependency Creates Structural Cost
GIS: 250k/yr vendors. Global SAP: 4–6 Accenture (+ turnover + offshore concerns). G&E: WIPRO handles L2. **Consequence**: vendor mix is not formally governed at the branch level — each team manages its own vendor relationship, creating cost-visibility gaps.

---

## Implications for the TOM

1. **Introduce a branch-level capacity planning ritual** (quarterly, not PDD-annual) covering all teams
2. **Mandate a single reporting stack** — Jira + one portfolio tool (PPM Now is the only candidate already in use)
3. **Define a minimum KPI set per team** (throughput · cycle time · WIP · incident MTTR · demand-vs-capacity index)
4. **Mitigate knowledge concentration** — document playbooks, rotate ownership, build shadow coverage
5. **Consolidate vendor governance** — branch-level vendor view, not team-by-team
