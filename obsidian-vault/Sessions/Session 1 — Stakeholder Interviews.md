## 17 Questions (from Workbook)
1. Role & Context — role + interaction with Digital Products + how it's changing
2. Strategic Priorities — top priorities for 6 months
3. Delivery Process — idea to delivery today
4. Pain Points — most friction or delays
5. Prioritization — department-level + personal-level
6. Tooling — Jira, ServiceNow, Excel, email
7. Visibility — how you see what's being worked on
8. One Fix — if you could fix one thing
9. Outcome Measurement — what makes this project a success
10. Governance — escalation when conflict
11. Information Gaps — what you need but don't have
12. Performance — how you measure value delivered
13. Alignment — BRS + consuming areas alignment
14. Decision Making — silos that need connecting
15. Pilot & Adoption — how a pilot could work
16. Risks — biggest risk of not changing
17. Open Input — anything we should know
