> 60 min group session with 5 teams. Round-robin facilitation.

## Canvas

## 6 Questions (per team)
1. What are your top priorities today?
2. What is your current Role and Responsibilities?
3. Which tools do you use? (Project management, Vendors, Development management)
4. What are your main pain points today?
5. How do you prioritize work today?
6. How do you receive demand requests?

## Timing
- 00: Introductions (5 min) — name, role, area
- 01–06: Questions (8 min each, round-robin) — ~48 min
- Wrap-up: 5 min
