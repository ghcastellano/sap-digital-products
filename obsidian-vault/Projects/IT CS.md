## Operating Model
- IT CS designs, builds, tests, and supports technology solutions
- Partners with Product Management and Business for delivery
- RACI model across Solution Design, Development, Testing, Deployment, Support

## Demand Flows
- **Small & Medium demand**: BSS Log → Jira → IT → Factory/Vendor
- **Large projects**: Annual Demand Planning (PDD) → Project Definition → Execution
- **Price Change process**: specific AS-IS → TO-BE transformation example
