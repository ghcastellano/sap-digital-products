# SAP Digital Products — Current Scope

#project #current #wave1

> **Our project** — Wave 1 of the transformation. Combines learnings from all 5 prior projects into a unified operating model for Digital Products at Avangrid.

## Context
- Digital Products faces rapid growth without a unified operating model
- 10+ demand channels, fragmented processes, no portfolio governance
- This project builds on [[P&S - P&T]], [[Digital CoE]], [[Renewables DTO]], [[Infrastructure]], and [[IT CS]]

---

## AS-IS Baseline

![[SAPDP_S6_Demand_Sources_AS_IS.png]]

**Demand Sources (AS-IS):** How work reaches Digital Products today:
- **10+ demand channels:** BRS, CX, Regulatory, Procurement, OT, IA, Data & Analytics, Infrastructure, P&T, Corporate, GIS
- **No single front door** — requests arrive via fragmented, unmonitored channels
- **No filtering** — low-value and strategic demands are mixed without initial triage
- Large demand organized annually through PDD Process
- BRS requests can go through ticketing system with Kanban management
- The Digital Products lifecycle begins formally at Intake (ideation & strategy occur upstream on Business side)

![[SAPDP_S8_Value_Criteria_ROI.png]]

**Value Criteria & ROI Definition:** Initial criteria draft for why prioritization matters — starting point for co-designing with stakeholders like [[Vitor]] and [[Carlos]].

![[SAPDP_S11_Wave1_Methodology.png]]

**Wave 1 Methodology:** Structured 5-step approach:
1. Review & Baseline (Weeks 1–2)
2. Define the Operating Model (Weeks 2–4)
3. Design the Tool Ecosystem (Weeks 4–7)
4. Pilot Execution with BRS (Weeks 7–13)
5. Learn & Adjust (Weeks 13–14)
