## The PBI Formula
Used by both [[P&S - P&T]] and [[Digital CoE]]:
$$PBI(x) = \frac{BV + UX}{E}$$
- **BV** = Business Value (1-10 scale)
- **UX** = User Experience value
- **E** = Effort / delivery cost
